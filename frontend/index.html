[ 02 ] // PROBLEM DOMAIN & THREAT ENVIRONMENT

The Escalating Crisis of Deceptive Digital Scams

Digital communications have become the primary battleground where malicious actors exploit human trust, urgency, and lack of technical inspection tools. CyberShield AI 2.0 addresses ten critical fraud vectors:

#01 THREAT VECTOR

Phishing Emails & Fake Portals

Deceptive emails mimicking legitimate institutions, banks, or services threatening account suspension to harvest passwords.

Credential Theft Brand Impersonation
#02 THREAT VECTOR

Fake Job & Work-From-Home Scams

Fraudulent high-paying daily task schemes demanding Telegram joins, social media liking tasks, and upfront fees.

Recruitment Fraud Telegram Redirection
#03 THREAT VECTOR

Fake Internship & Training Fee Fraud

Bogus offer letters sent to students requiring upfront "training fees", certificate charges, or security deposits.

Student Exploitation Fee Traps
#04 THREAT VECTOR

SMS Smishing & Electricity Disconnection

Urgent SMS claiming power will be disconnected at 9:30 PM due to unpaid bills, prompting calls to rogue officer numbers.

Utility Smishing Artificial Panic
#05 THREAT VECTOR

UPI PIN & QR Code Fraud

Manipulating victims into entering their UPI PIN or scanning a merchant QR code under the false pretense of "receiving money".

Payment Reversal QR Code Exploits
#06 THREAT VECTOR

OTP Theft & Remote Desktop Abuse

Coercing users to share 6-digit OTPs or install screen-sharing software (AnyDesk, TeamViewer) to drain bank accounts.

Remote Access OTP Solicitation
#07 THREAT VECTOR

Lookalike Domains & IP Hostnames

URLs using raw IP addresses, suspicious top-level domains (.xyz, .cfd), or Punycode IDN homographs to spoof brands.

Punycode Spoofing Obfuscated Links
#08 THREAT VECTOR

Digital Arrest & Authority Coercion

Scammers impersonating CBI, Police, or Customs claiming illicit drug parcels, demanding video confinement and fund transfers.

Extortion Legal Intimidation
#09 THREAT VECTOR

Fragmented & Opaque Threat Warnings

Users receive vague browser blocks without clear evidence, actionable countermeasures, or plain-language explanations.

Usability Gap Opaque Risk
#10 THREAT VECTOR

Advanced Adversarial Prompt Injections

Attackers embed hidden system override instructions ("Ignore previous prompts") inside scams to fool modern AI models.

LLM Jailbreak Instruction Hijack

[ 03 ] // ARCHITECTURAL SOLUTION & DEFENSE PIPELINE

Deterministic Rigor Meets Contextual AI Reasoning

CyberShield AI 2.0 does not blindly trust probabilistic LLM outputs. We enforce a 6-layer zero-trust pipeline separating strictly observed empirical evidence from generative explanations:

01

User Ingestion

Untrusted text, SMS, email, URL, or file uploaded into the boundary.

02

PII Sanitization

Client & server redaction of credit cards, Aadhaar, PAN, UPI, passwords, and OTPs.

03

Prompt Guard

Adversarial instruction overrides and jailbreaks quarantined before AI processing.

04

IOC Extraction

Deterministic extraction of URLs, domains, phones, UPI VPAs, and network anomalies.

05

AI & Heuristics

Gemini 3.8 contextual reasoning with instantaneous heuristic failover on timeout.

06

Evidence & Action

Normalized score (0-100), categorical risk level, empirical evidence, and mitigation steps.

[ LAYER A ] DETERMINISTIC SECURITY ENGINE MATHEMATICAL CERTAINTY
  • Zero hallucination: observed indicators strictly exist in input.
  • Magic byte verification: PE binaries disguised as PDFs detected instantly.
  • Zip-Bomb & Zip-Slip traversal rejection without code execution.
  • Luhn algorithm verification for credit and debit card numbers.
  • Operates in < 15ms even when network or AI providers are completely offline.
[ LAYER B ] GOOGLE GEMINI 3.8 AI REASONING CONTEXTUAL INTELLIGENCE
  • Decodes complex social engineering manipulation and psychological urgency.
  • Evaluates nuanced career and recruitment communication contexts.
  • Synthesizes plain-language explanations for non-technical users.
  • Strict JSON schema enforcement with bounded 10-second timeout.
  • Safe automatic fallback: network failure never crashes the security verdict.
